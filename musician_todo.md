# Musician Task List - KKJam

## Overview
The game relies on **Vertical Layering**. We need 4 instrument stems that can play simultaneously and sound good in any combination.
**BPM**: 100 (Base). The game will pitch-shift this up, so ensure samples sound okay when sped up.
**Key**: C Major or G Major (something happy and universally consonant).
**Loop Length**: 4 Bars (approx 9.6 seconds at 100 BPM).

---

## The Stems (Loops)
Each instrument needs 3 levels of intensity.
*Note: In the game, only the highest level of each type plays. E.g., if there is a Lvl 3 Bunny, we play the Lvl 3 Drum loop.*

### 1. Drums (Bunny)
- **Level 1**: Simple kick/snap or light percussion. Sparse.
- **Level 2**: Basic rock/pop beat. Kick, snare, hi-hat.
- **Level 3**: Full energy groove. Fills, crash cymbals, driving rhythm.

### 2. Bass (Frog)
- **Level 1**: Simple root notes on the downbeat. (Dum... Dum...)
- **Level 2**: Walking bass or simple groove.
- **Level 3**: Funky, slap bass, or driving synth bass. Busy and cool.

### 3. Melody (Cat)
- **Level 1**: Occasional cute motifs or simple chiptune beeps.
- **Level 2**: A clear, catchy melody line.
- **Level 3**: Full lead synth or piano. Harmonized and rich.

### 4. Harmony (Bird)
- **Level 1**: Soft pads or light humming. Background texture.
- **Level 2**: Chords or backing vocals ("Oooohs").
- **Level 3**: Arpeggios or full choir/brass section hits.

---

## The "Ultimate Concert"
- **Transition Stinger**: A sound to play when the 4th Level 3 hits the stage.
- **Concert Loop**: A special 8-bar loop that plays when the "Album" is being recorded. This can be a pre-mixed "Perfect Version" of the song with extra crowd noise and fireworks SFX baked in.

---

## Sound Effects (SFX)
- **Swap**: A quick "woosh" or "click".
- **Match/Merge (Lvl 1->2)**: Satisfying "pop" or major chord triad.
- **Match/Merge (Lvl 2->3)**: Louder, more magical "shimmer" sound.
- **Fly to Stage**: A rising slide whistle or "ding!".
- **Shuffle**: A deck shuffle sound or rapid scale run.
- **Game Over (Stretch Goal)**: A "record scratch" or crowd "awww".
