# Illustrator Task List - KKJam

## Character Design (The Band)
We need 4 distinct critters. Each critter needs 3 evolution stages.
**Style**: Minimalist, flat colors, cute/chibi aesthetic. Readable at small sizes (grid is 8x8).

### 1. Drummer Bunny (Percussion)
- **Color**: Red/Pink theme.
- **Level 1 (Baby)**: Small, holding a tiny stick or tapping foot.
- **Level 2 (Teen)**: Wearing sunglasses, holding snare drum.
- **Level 3 (Star)**: Full drum kit, flashy outfit, glowing aura.

### 2. Melody Cat (Melody)
- **Color**: Blue/Cyan theme.
- **Level 1 (Baby)**: Meowing or holding a toy whistle.
- **Level 2 (Teen)**: Holding a keyboard or keytar.
- **Level 3 (Star)**: DJ setup or grand piano, cool hat.

### 3. Bass Frog (Bass)
- **Color**: Green theme.
- **Level 1 (Baby)**: Tadpole-ish, plucking a rubber band.
- **Level 2 (Teen)**: Slapping a simple bass guitar.
- **Level 3 (Star)**: Funky double bass or 5-string electric, sunglasses.

### 4. Harmony Bird (Harmony)
- **Color**: Yellow/Orange theme.
- **Level 1 (Baby)**: Chirping, small beak open.
- **Level 2 (Teen)**: Singing into a microphone.
- **Level 3 (Star)**: Diva outfit, multiple microphones or backup dancers (tiny birds).

---

## Animations
Each critter needs these states (can be simple 2-4 frame loops or tweens):
- **Idle**: Bouncing to the beat (BPM will vary, so keep it loopable).
- **Selected**: Wiggle or highlight when clicked.
- **Merge**: A "poof" or "spin" effect when 3 combine.
- **Fly to Stage**: A pose for when the Level 3 flies up to the UI.

---

## UI Assets
- **The Grid**: A clean 8x8 board background (checkerboard or slots).
- **The Stage**: A spotlight area above the grid where the 4 Level 3s will stand.
- **Shuffle Button**: A distinct button icon (arrows/dice).
- **Album Counter**: A vinyl record icon or number display.
- **Particles**:
  - Sparkles for merging.
  - Musical notes floating up.
  - Fireworks for the "Concert" event.

---

## Backgrounds
- **Main Game**: Simple gradient or subtle pattern (don't distract from grid).
- **Concert Mode**: A variation of the background (darker with spotlights?) for when the Ultimate Song plays.
